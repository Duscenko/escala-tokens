# DEC-2026-11-17-001 · In Dark, the secondary button sits two steps above the card

- **Date:** 2026-11-17 · **Owner:** Cesar Durango · **Status:** Decided

## Decision
On a dark card the secondary solid button barely showed: its fill (`Gray Dark/04`) against
`Surface/layer-2` (`Gray Dark/03`) read 1.09:1, so the only edge was its 2px top highlight,
which looked like a half border ("View report" on the stat card, "Upload files"). In the web
generator it was worse: the styles' `DARK_DEPTH` lifts layer-2 to tone 4, the button's own
tone, so the two were identical (`#222223`).

Decided in the Figma file first. An experiment on the Button page (section
"🧪 Experimento · Botón secundario en Dark") compared tones 04 · 05 · 06 over page,
layer-1 and layer-2 in Escala Dark. The owner chose **C · Gray Dark/06**. The file was then
changed and the code follows:

| Role | Mode | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|---|
| `action.secondary.default` | Dark | `Gray Dark/04` · `{neutral-dark.4}` | `Gray Dark/06` · `{neutral-dark.6}` |
| `action.secondary.hover` | Dark | `Gray Dark/05` · `{neutral-dark.5}` | `Gray Dark/07` · `{neutral-dark.7}` |
| `action.secondary.pressed` | Dark | `Gray Dark/06` · `{neutral-dark.6}` | `Gray Dark/08` · `{neutral-dark.8}` |

Light is unchanged.

Measured (Figma palette, WCAG): tone 06 reads 1.58 on the page, 1.48 on layer-1 and
1.38 on layer-2, against 1.24 · 1.17 · 1.09 for tone 04.

## Consequence
- In the web styles, layer-2 sits on tone 4, so the button is again two steps above it with
  no style override. The teal style (`abyss-neon`), whose layer-2 sits on 5, pins the button
  to 7 · 8 · 9. `themePresets.test.ts` holds every style to "two steps above layer-2 in Dark"
  and "never the colour of a surface".
- Store v152 migrates saved brands whose Dark layer-2 is lifted past what the schema clears and
  that have no hand-picked secondary fill. Published systems pick it up on their next save.
- The library must be **published** from Figma for other files to receive the new aliases.
