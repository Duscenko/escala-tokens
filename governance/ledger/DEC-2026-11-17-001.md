# DEC-2026-11-17-001 · The secondary button sits two steps above the card, Light and Dark

- **Date:** 2026-11-17 · **Owner:** Cesar Durango · **Status:** Decided

## Decision
On a card the secondary solid button barely showed. Its fill sat one step above
`Surface/layer-2`: 1.09:1 in Dark (`Gray Dark/04` on `/03`) and 1.11–1.15:1 in Light in
every web style (green, pink and lavender brands the worst). The only edge was its top
highlight, which read as a half border. In the web generator Dark was worse: the styles'
`DARK_DEPTH` lifts layer-2 to tone 4, the button's own tone (`#222223` on `#222223`).

Decided in the Figma file first, on the Button page (sections "🧪 Experimento · Botón
secundario en Dark / Light"):

- **Dark:** option C, `Gray Dark/06`.
- **Light:** a border (rejected: "a border in one appearance and not the other is not
  consistent") and a translucent fill were shown. The owner chose the simplest rule: the
  resting fill takes the tone hover had, and hover · pressed walk on up the ramp.
- **No borders**, in either appearance.

| Role | Light was → now | Dark was → now |
|---|---|---|
| `action.secondary.default` | `Gray/04` → **`Gray/05`** | `Gray Dark/04` → **`Gray Dark/06`** |
| `action.secondary.hover` | `Gray/05` → **`Gray/06`** | `Gray Dark/05` → **`Gray Dark/07`** |
| `action.secondary.pressed` | `Gray/06` → **`Gray/07`** | `Gray Dark/06` → **`Gray Dark/08`** |

Both appearances now sit two steps above layer-2. On layer-2 the resting button reads
1.26–1.34:1 in Light (18 styles) and 1.38:1 in Dark (file palette).

## Consequence
- **Light labels.** Resting: AA and Lc 64–70. Hover (Gray/06): AA but Lc ~57. Pressed
  (Gray/07): ~4.2:1 in the default neutrals. Both are transient, so they are audited as
  `pressed-label` (WCAG 3:1, no APCA term), covered by `EXC-2026-11-17-001`.
- **Web styles.** No style override is needed (layer-2 on 4 in Dark, the button on 6). The
  teal style (`abyss-neon`), whose Dark layer-2 sits on 5, pins the button to 7 · 8 · 9.
  `themePresets.test.ts` holds every style to "two steps above layer-2" in Light and Dark,
  and to "never the colour of a surface".
- **Saved brands.** Store v152 migrates brands whose Dark layer-2 sits past what the schema
  clears. Published systems pick it up on their next save.
- **Library.** It must be **published** from Figma for other files to get the new aliases.
