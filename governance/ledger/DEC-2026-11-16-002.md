# DEC-2026-11-16-002 · Radius ladder 0 · 4 · 8 · 12 · 20 · 24 · full

- **Date:** 2026-11-16 · **Owner:** Cesar Durango · **Status:** Decided (made Figma-first, ported to code in this loop)

## Context
`Radius/control` was 6 (off the 4 px grid) and `container` / `overlay` shared 16, so two roles had one value and the ladder had no room for concentric nesting.

## Decision
Every step on the 4 px grid, one job each, no two roles with the same value:

| Role | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| `radius.none` | 0 | 0 |
| `radius.control` | 6 (Figma) / 8 (code) | 4 |
| `radius.item` | n/a | 8 (DEC-001) |
| `radius.action` | 12 | 12 |
| `radius.container` | 16 | 20 |
| `radius.overlay` | 16 | 24 |
| `radius.pill` | full | full |

Avatar, Chip, FeaturedIcon and Tooltip stay on `radius.action` (12) so they do not turn into circles. Overlay 24 − `inset-control` 12 = 12 = `radius.action` (concentric rows).

## Consequence
`radius.container` = 20 has no named step on the Tailwind ramp: roles pin the Dimension primitive `dimension-20`. Contrast and layout goldens move; component recipes that hold `container` / `overlay` follow automatically.
