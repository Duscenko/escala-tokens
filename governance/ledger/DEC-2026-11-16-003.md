# DEC-2026-11-16-003 · Soft container borders, Switch off thumb

- **Date:** 2026-11-16 · **Owner:** Cesar Durango · **Status:** Decided (Figma-first, ported in this loop)

## Decision
1. **Container borders are soft.** `Border/default` = `Black Alpha/02` Light · `White Alpha/02` Dark (was 03). `Border/subtle` = `Black Alpha/01` · `White Alpha/01` (was 02). Edges of cards, overlays, toasts and alerts are not control boundaries (WCAG 1.4.11 does not apply; shadow and surface carry the separation). `Border/control` is unchanged by this decision.
2. **Switch off thumb** = `Content/secondary` over `Surface/muted` (was `Surface/input`, 1.28:1; now 4.2:1 Light, 7.4:1 Dark). The `io` glyph takes the track role. Disabled thumb (both states) = `Content/disabled`.

## Consequence
Never use `Border/default` as the only boundary of a control. `Border/control` at `Black Alpha/03` is below 3:1: finding `COL-002`, `needs-decision` (see issue).
