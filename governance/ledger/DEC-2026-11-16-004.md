# DEC-2026-11-16-004 · The Figma file is the rule: `Border/control` stays at Alpha/03

- **Date:** 2026-11-16 · **Owner:** Cesar Durango · **Status:** Decided (Accepted deviation from WCAG 1.4.11)
- **Finding:** COL-002 (issue #12), closed as `accepted`.

## Decision
The decisions the owner took in Figma are the ones that go into code. `Border/control` = `Black Alpha/03` Light · `White Alpha/03` Dark, hover `/04`: a soft hairline (1.41:1 / 1.51:1), below the 3:1 of WCAG 1.4.11 for a control boundary. The code is aligned to the file (it was solved to 3:1 at Alpha/07 · 06).

## Consequence
- `border.control` / `border.control-hover` are pinned to those steps, not solved. New audit intent `control-hairline` (visibility floor 1.4:1).
- A control identified by its stroke alone no longer clears 1.4.11. Better practice, for the owner to weigh later: give a bare field a visible fill, or offer a high-contrast mode. Not done.
- `Border/default` and `Border/subtle` stay soft by DEC-2026-11-16-003.
