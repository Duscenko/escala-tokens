# DEC-2026-11-18-010 · NavItem trailing chevron

- **Date:** 2026-11-18 · **Owner:** Cesar Durango · **Status:** Decided

## Decision
The trailing chevron is one step below the leading icon: `Size/icon-sm` on `sm` and `default`, `Size/icon-md` on `lg`. Ink `Content/secondary`, `Content/disabled` when Disabled. Header and floating navigation use `NavItem Size=sm`.
