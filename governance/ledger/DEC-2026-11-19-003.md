# DEC-2026-11-19-003 · ReactionGroup: the pills are Chip, the press is motion

- **Date:** 2026-11-19 · **Owner:** Cesar Durango · **Status:** Decided
- **Source:** Figma → Storybook handoff 2026-11-19, §3 and §7

## Decision
1. Reaction pills are the existing Chip (`appearance="outline"`, `shape="pill"`, Selected when you reacted). No new pill component.
2. ReactionGroupTrigger stays in its family. It is promoted to `IconButton Shape=pill` only when a second consumer appears.
3. Chip gets no `Pressed` state in Figma: press feedback is motion (`use-press`).

## Consequence
- Code: `src/components/escala-ui/reaction-group.tsx`, recipes `ReactionGroup` · `ReactionGroupTrigger` · `ReactionGroupPopover` (`lib/recipes/actions.ts`),
  contract `design-plans/reaction-group-contract.md`.
- Chip: `RECIPE_MOTION.Chip` gains `press` (every Chip, not only reaction pills) and a `toggleLabel` prop for a toggle whose visible label is a bare count.
- Recipe motion gains `stagger` (an item waits its index times `--motion-stagger`); ReactionGroup is its first user.
