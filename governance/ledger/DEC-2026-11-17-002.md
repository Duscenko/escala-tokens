# DEC-2026-11-17-002 · The tag family leaves `Radius/control` for `Radius/item`

- **Date:** 2026-11-17 · **Owner:** Cesar Durango · **Status:** Decided
- **Finding:** `FIG-002:Radius/control-overloaded` · plan `design-plans/component-box-classification.md` §4.4

## Context
`Radius/control` (Selectors axis in the generator) was bound by ~45 sets with unrelated jobs. Raising it
for rounder badges turned a 16 px Checkbox into a circle that reads as a Radio. Shown before / after on
the Figma page `🧪 Experiments`
([board](https://www.figma.com/design/7j5zukN5HpihGVJMmyjA20/?node-id=2059-172)).

## Options
- **(a)** Move the tag family to the existing `Radius/item` (Items axis). ← chosen
- (b) Add a new tag role in the file.
- (c) Keep it and rely on the Checkbox cap in the recipe (§4.2).

## Decision
Option (a), approved by the owner after seeing the board.

| Piece | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Badge `Shape=rounded` (root) | `Radius/control` | `Radius/item` |
| Kbd (every size) | `Radius/control` | `Radius/item` |
| InputTagChip (root + `focus-ring`) | `Radius/control` | `Radius/item` |

Figma: 305 layers rebound in the three main components; every instance (Input › Kbd, InputTag › tag,
AppHeader, AnnouncementBar, ReleaseModal…) followed with no override. Stay on `Radius/control`:
Checkbox family, Slider, TextLink / Breadcrumb focus rings, media.

## Consequence
- Default look changes: these parts go from 4 px to 8 px (`Radius/item` today).
- Code: `BADGE` (rounded), `KBD`, `INPUT` › `kbd`, `INPUT_TAG_CHIP` read `radius.item`; contracts and
  goldens regenerated. Generator hints: Items = "segment, sidebar menu button, badge, kbd, tag";
  Selectors = "checkbox, slider, link focus ring".
- Rollback: rebind those three sets back to `Radius/control` by name (no named version could be saved
  through the MCP; this table is the revert).
