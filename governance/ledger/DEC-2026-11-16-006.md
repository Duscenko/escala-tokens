# DEC-2026-11-16-006 · The Figma file is the rule: three colour roles and the shadow colours follow the file

- **Date:** 2026-11-16 · **Owner:** Cesar Durango · **Status:** Decided

## Decision
After DEC-2026-11-16-004 the owner kept curating colours in Figma. A read-only comparison of every Color Semantics role (83 × Light and Dark), the 341 Color Primitives and the 45 Social Media Brands variables against Storybook found three roles and the shadow colours out of step. The code is aligned to the file ("alínealo", 2026-11-16):

| Role | Mode | Was | Now (the file) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `status.critical.content` | Dark | `{error.11}` `#ff8b7c` | `{error.10}` `#fb493d` |
| `border.control` | Dark | `{white-a.3}` `#ffffff26` | `{white-a.4}` `#ffffff33` |
| `elevation.highlight` | Light | `{white-a.3}` `#ffffff26` | `{white-a.5}` `#ffffff4d` |

The `--shadow-*` ramp reads the `Elevation/*` roles (ring · key · ambient), as the file's `Shadow/*` effect styles do, instead of its own `rgba(10,13,18,…)` ramp.

## Consequence
- In Dark, `border.control` and `border.control-hover` are now the same step (`White Alpha/04`): a field's resting and hover outline are identical. Flagged to the owner; kept because the file is the rule.
- Dark `status.critical.content` on its own tint reads Lc ~42, under the `large-text` floor. The same figure was why the role had been moved to 11. Covered by an exception.
- Pinned to the file's steps, not solved. `COL-002` exception `EXC-2026-11-16-001` is widened to the Dark `Border/control` step.
