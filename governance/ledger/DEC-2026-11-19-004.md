# DEC-2026-11-19-004 · Auth setup blocks: controlled by Type × Status, nested parts keep their own motion

- **Date:** 2026-11-19 · **Owner:** Cesar Durango · **Status:** Decided (items 2 and 3 are the build's reading of the handoff: confirm or change)
- **Source:** Figma → Storybook handoff 2026-11-19, §4, §5 and §7

## Decision
1. `AuthPasskeySetup` and `AuthTwoFactorSetup` are controlled blocks: `type` × `status` are the Figma axes and every action is a callback.
   The flow lives in two hooks (`useAuthPasskeySetup({ create, save })`, `useAuthTwoFactorSetup({ verify })`); the app supplies only
   `navigator.credentials.create()` or the code check. A pair the file does not draw shows the nearest one.
2. A component nested in a block keeps its own motion role. The handoff asked for `use-travel-in` on the progress segments and
   `use-color` on Turn on; the build leaves `ProgressLine` on `use-progress` and `Button` on `use-hover`, because one component
   must not move differently from block to block and a block must not put a second transition on a part a recipe already times.
   The block's own moments use the roles the handoff names: step swap `use-page-transition`, inline error `use-disclosure-in`,
   waiting ring `use-loop`, check `use-emphasis-in`.
3. The QR is generated in code with `qrcode-generator` 2.0.4 (MIT, no dependencies, pinned), not `qrcode`, which installs
   `pngjs` and `yargs`. Error correction M, 37 modules for the sample link.
4. `AuthTwoFactorSetupItem` and the "Simulated…" helpers are demo material: a live product passes `authenticatorCode={null}`
   and its own `helper`. The mocked authenticators live in `blocks/authSetupDemo.tsx`, outside the blocks.

## Consequence
- Code: `src/components/escala-ui/blocks/authSetup.tsx` (+ `authSetupDemo.tsx`), contract `blocks/authSetup.contract.json`,
  guard `src/lib/__tests__/authSetupBlocks.test.ts`, stories `Blocks/Auth/PasskeySetup` and `Blocks/Auth/TwoFactorSetup`,
  platform entries in `configurator/blocks/blockRegistry.tsx`.
- `type.code` joins `BLOCK_ONLY_REFS` (no recipe reads it yet); Storybook's scope loads the mono family.
- Accepted exceptions: EXC-2026-11-19-003 (QR black on white), EXC-2026-11-19-004 (left-aligned ModalHeader).
- The step region is a minimum height, not a fixed one: equal on the 640 card, and it grows when a phone wraps the text.
