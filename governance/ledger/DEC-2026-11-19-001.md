# DEC-2026-11-19-001 · Menu rows focus like buttons

- **Date:** 2026-11-19 · **Owner:** Cesar Durango · **Status:** Decided
- **Finding:** PAR-001:DropdownMenuItem:State=Focused/ring

## Context
The Button rule of 2026-11-17 is ONE focus ring, `Stroke/focus` wide, 1px off the control, in the intent's colour, with nothing inside. DropdownMenuItem
still drew the older focus in both Figma and code: a 2px INSIDE edge (`Border/focus` · `Status/critical/border-strong`) plus a halo of the same bounds.

## Options
1. Keep the old focus on menu rows (Figma and code already agree).
2. Menu rows follow the Button rule (the gap ring).

## Decision
Option 2. A focused DropdownMenuItem is its highlighted fill plus the gap ring: `Border/ring/default` on default, `Status/critical/ring` on critical.

## Consequence
- Code: `listItemRecipe(..., { focusRing: 'gap' })` for DropdownMenuItem (`recipes/listItem.ts`, `recipes/navigation.ts`). The panel's inset holds the 3px the ring needs.
- ComboboxItem and SidebarMenuButton keep their own Figma focus for now (the shared `listItemRecipe` default stays `edge`); bring them in by passing `focusRing: 'gap'` once their sets change.
- Figma: the DropdownMenuItem `Focused` variants are to be updated by the owner (drop the INSIDE stroke, move `focus-ring` 1px out, critical ring `Status/critical/ring`). Until then the daily review reports this difference as decided, not as drift.
