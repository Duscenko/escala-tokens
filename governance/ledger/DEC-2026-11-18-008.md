# DEC-2026-11-18-008 · Escala Dark borders are soft

- **Date:** 2026-11-18 · **Owner:** Cesar Durango · **Status:** Decided
- **Findings:** COL-002, COL-003

## Context
The Dark borders read harsh next to real components.

## Options (compared on canvas with real components)
| Option | `subtle` · `default` · `control` | `control-hover` · `strong` |
|---|---|---|
| A | 10 % · 15 % · 20 % | up to 60 % |
| **B (chosen)** | 5 % · 5 % · 5 % | 10 % · 15 % |
| C | 5 % · 10 % · 15 % | 20 % |

## Decision
Escala Dark: `Border/subtle`, `Border/default` and `Border/control` = White Alpha/01, `Border/control-hover` = /02, `Border/strong` = /03. Light does not change.

## Consequence
`subtle` and `default` share a value in Dark on purpose. `Border/control` Dark is below the 3:1 of WCAG 1.4.11: accepted in EXC-2026-11-16-001 until the high-contrast mode exists. The contrast audit holds these strokes to a visibility floor (`dark-soft-border`, 1.05:1).
