# DEC-2026-11-18-009 · Track and thumb never share a step

- **Date:** 2026-11-18 · **Owner:** Cesar Durango · **Status:** Decided

## Context
At Gray Dark/04 `Surface/raised` equalled `Surface/muted`, so the selected segment of a SegmentedControl disappeared in Dark.

## Decision
`Surface/muted` Light = Gray/03, `Surface/muted-hover` Light = Gray/04, `Surface/raised` Dark = Gray Dark/06 (restores DEC-2026-11-17-003).

## Consequence
New invariant, held by `figmaFilePalette.test.ts`: `Surface/raised` and `Surface/muted` differ in both modes.
