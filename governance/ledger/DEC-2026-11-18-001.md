## 1 · Who owns motion
Amends DEC-2026-11-16-007. **Figma owns the Motion collection's names, values and curves. Code owns how they play**
(keyframes, timers, pause, compilation).

## 3 · New tokens (Motion collection 79 → 98, roles 24 → 30)
| Kind | Token | Value |

Choreographies `toast-enter`, `toast-exit`, `toast-countdown`. `sonner-enter` stays published (tokens.json is additive);
no component plays it. Store v154 gives saved systems the new roles from the preset they were on.

## 4 · How the Toast behaves
- **The bar is the clock.** It empties with `scaleX`, linearly; when it ends the toast closes. Sonner's timer is off
  (`duration: Infinity`), so bar and close cannot drift apart. Hover, focus, swipe and a hidden page pause it.
- **Lifetime follows content:** one line 4 s, with a description 6 s, with an action 10 s.
- **A critical toast does not close by itself** and shows no bar.
- **Exit fits the queue:** `use-notify-out` stays at or under Sonner's 200 ms unmount in all five presets (Calm uses `quick`).
- **Reduced motion:** a 160 ms fade, no travel or scale; the countdown keeps running because it is information.

## Not done

  A plugin sync of Motion must not run until that generator is aligned.
- Figma prototype transitions cannot read variables: `Motion/Toast/*` styles carry the values of the roles.
