# DEC-2026-11-18-007 · Surface inset by viewport, `inset-action`, brand focus rings

- **Date:** 2026-11-18 · **Owner:** Cesar Durango · **Status:** Decided
- **From:** the [comparison board](https://www.figma.com/design/7j5zukN5HpihGVJMmyjA20/?node-id=2184-5937), decisions 4, 5 and 6.

| Token | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| `spacing.inset-surface` | 20 on every viewport | 24 · 20 · 16 |
| `spacing.inset-action` (new) | none, buttons read `inset-control` 12 | 16 · 16 · 12, on Button and SocialLoginButton `default` and `lg`; `link`, `xs` and `sm` keep 12 |
| `--social-<brand>-border-focus` | solid black or white, not drawn | brand at 31 %; `Border/ring/neutral` on Apple, GitHub and X |
| SocialLoginButton Focused | `Border/ring/default` halo | one 2px ring 1px outside the edge, in `<Brand>/Border/focus` |

`tokens.json` stays additive: `socialBrands.*.focus` keeps its value and `focusRing` is new. A saved system that already holds its own surface inset keeps it. The spacing modes follow the new default: Quiet and Bold 24, Airy 32, Compact 12.

## Label of SocialLoginButton
The label follows the file: 14/21 at `default` and 16/25 at `lg` (`size/text-md` · `text-lg`), with the family and weight of `Type/button`. It read 12/18.
