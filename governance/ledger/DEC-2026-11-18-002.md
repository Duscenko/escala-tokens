# DEC-2026-11-18-002 · Dark inputs calibration: five Color Semantics roles

- **Date:** 2026-11-18 · **Owner:** Cesar Durango · **Status:** Decided
- **Figma:** [Storybook sync · 18 Nov 2026](https://www.figma.com/design/7j5zukN5HpihGVJMmyjA20/?node-id=2147-4874)

## 1 · What moved
| Role | Mode | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Surface/raised` | Escala Dark | Gray Dark/06 | Gray Dark/04 (#252428) |
| `Surface/input` | Escala Dark | Accents Dark/01 | Gray Dark/02 (#18161a) |
| `Border/control` | Escala Dark | White Alpha/04 | White Alpha/03 |
| `Content/subtle` | Escala Dark | Gray Dark/09 | Gray Dark/10 (#78767f) |
| `Content/subtle` | Escala Light | Gray/09 | Gray/10 (#898494) |

Unchanged: `Border/control` Light, `Border/control-hover` in both themes, `Surface/input` Light.

## 2 · Measured (`darkInputsCalibration.test.ts`)
Dark field on `surface.layer-2` 1.07:1, on `surface.raised` 1.17:1. `content.subtle` on `surface.input`: Light 3.6:1, Dark 4.0:1.
`border.control`: Light 1.41:1, Dark 1.57:1. In Dark, rest (/03) and hover (/04) now differ.

## 3 · Accepted deviation
Control borders stay under the 3:1 of WCAG 1.4.11 in the default themes, by owner decision (EXC-2026-11-16-001). Strong
borders belong to the future high-contrast mode, so COL-002 stays open for that mode.

## 4 · Consequence to review
`Surface/raised` and `Surface/muted` are now the same step in Dark (Gray Dark/04). The selected SegmentedControl segment
binds the first and its track the second, so in Dark the selection only reads through its shadow. Built as the file has it.
