## Usage

Use for the one primary action of a screen that floats over its content (create, compose, add). Give an icon-only FAB an aria-label. A FAB inside a row of actions is a Button; several floating actions together are a FAB Group.

## Accessibility

Native `<button>`. With a label that is the accessible name; an icon-only FAB needs an aria-label. Disabled sets aria-disabled. Focus draws a 2px halo outside the button; the elevation stays.
