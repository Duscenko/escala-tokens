## Behaviour

**Dialog** (radix-ui, dialog-modal). Focus moves into the dialog on open, is trapped while open and returns to the trigger on close; the page behind is inert.
- `Escape` closes the dialog and returns focus to the trigger
- `Tab / Shift+Tab` cycles focus inside the dialog (focus trap)

ARIA in the source: aria-label

## Usage

Use for secondary tasks that keep the page in context (filters, a cart, details, settings). For a blocking decision use Modal or Alert Dialog; for a list of choices tied to a field use Popover.

## Accessibility

A Radix Dialog like Modal: focus is trapped inside, Esc closes and focus returns to the trigger. The header's title is the accessible name and its description the aria-describedby; the close button has an accessible name. The bottom side is capped by the visual viewport (not 100vh) and keeps the safe area under its footer.
