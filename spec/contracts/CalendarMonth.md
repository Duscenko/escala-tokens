## Behaviour

**Own source** (grid (date picker dialog, APG)). One day is the tab stop (the selection, else today, else the first enabled day of the month); the arrows move between the others (roving tabindex).
- `ArrowLeft / ArrowRight` moves focus one day back / forward, skipping disabled days
- `ArrowUp / ArrowDown` moves focus one week back / forward, skipping disabled days
- `Enter / Space` selects the focused day (native button)
- Source: src/components/escala-ui/calendar.tsx · CalendarMonth onKeyDown + tabbable · story `CalendarMonthPlayground`

ARIA in the source: aria-disabled · aria-label · aria-pressed

## Usage

Use inside Calendar below the header. single and multiple mark chosen days, range marks a span with its band; secondary swaps the selected look for the inverse one.

## Accessibility

Each day is a button (see CalendarDay); Disabled and Unavailable days are disabled. The weekday row is plain text.
