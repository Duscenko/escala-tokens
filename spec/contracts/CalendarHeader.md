## Usage

Use at the top of Calendar. single opens one month-and-year picker, multiple opens the month and the year separately, plain is a static heading for a calendar with no picker.

## Accessibility

The arrows are buttons named "Previous month" and "Next month"; each chip is a button whose visible text is its name.
