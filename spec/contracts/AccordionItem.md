## Motion

- `chevron`: transform → `use-disclosure-in`
- enter: `accordion-expand` on `content`
- exit: `accordion-collapse` on `content`

## Behaviour

**Accordion** (radix-ui, accordion). Each trigger is a button in the tab order; the panel is labelled by its trigger.
- `Space / Enter` toggles the focused section
- `ArrowDown / ArrowUp` moves focus to the next / previous trigger
- `Home / End` moves focus to the first / last trigger

## Usage

Always inside an Accordion; give every item a value.

## Accessibility

A button in a heading with aria-expanded and aria-controls; Disabled sets disabled and mutes icon, label and chevron.
