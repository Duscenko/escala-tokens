## Behaviour

**Toaster** (sonner, alert (live region)). Toasts announce through a polite live region; hover or focus pauses the timer.
- `Alt+T` moves focus to the toast region (sonner hotkey)

ARIA in the source: aria-label · aria-live

## Usage

Use for transient feedback (saved, copied, undone). Keep the title under six words; an action such as Undo needs a second way to be reached because the toast times out. It rises into place, its bar shows the time left, and it closes when the bar runs out: 4 s for one line, 6 s with a description, 10 s with an action. Hover or focus pause it. A critical toast stays until it is closed. For a persistent notice use Alert, for form validation Field.

## Accessibility

role="status" (polite), and role="alert" for critical. Do not rely on colour alone: the icon and the text carry the severity. The time left pauses on hover, on focus and while the page is hidden; a critical toast never closes by itself. With reduced motion it fades instead of moving, and the bar keeps running because it is information.
