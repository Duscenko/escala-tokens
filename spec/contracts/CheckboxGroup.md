## Behaviour

**Checkbox** (radix-ui, checkbox). The box is one tab stop; its Label is the accessible name.
- `Space` toggles checked (indeterminate → checked)

ARIA in the source: role=checkbox · role=group · aria-describedby · aria-disabled · aria-invalid · aria-labelledby

## Usage

Use for several independent options under one question. Invalid shows the footer as the error; Disabled mutes the whole group. Size, Placement, State and Variant cascade to every row.

## Accessibility

role="group" with aria-labelledby on the group label and aria-describedby on the footer; each checkbox stays independent.
