## Behaviour

**Own source** (grid (data grid)). One roving tab stop in a role="grid"; every cell names its day and count, so the level never relies on colour.
- `ArrowLeft / ArrowRight` moves one week back / forward
- `ArrowUp / ArrowDown` moves one day up / down within the week
- `Home / End` moves to the first / last day of the week
- `Ctrl+Home / Ctrl+End` moves to the first / last day of the grid
- Source: src/components/escala-ui/activity-heatmap.tsx · onKeyDown → lib/activityHeatmap.ts moveActivityFocus · story `Playground`

ARIA in the source: role=grid · role=gridcell · role=presentation · role=row · aria-colcount · aria-colindex · aria-label · aria-rowcount · aria-rowindex

## Usage

Contribution and habit calendars: one number per day over a year. Year switching is a SegmentedControl outside the component; the focused day is shown in a caption below by the consumer (onFocusDay). For one value over time use Sparkline; for a share of a whole, SplitBar. Related: ActivityHeatmapCell, SegmentedControl.

## Accessibility

role="grid" with 7 rows (weekdays) of gridcells and one roving tab stop. Left/Right move by week, Up/Down by day, Home/End to the first/last day of the week, Ctrl+Home/End to the first/last day of the grid. Every cell is named by its day and count, so the level never relies on colour alone. The legend and the labels are decorative (aria-hidden).
