## Behaviour

**Slider** (radix-ui, slider). Each thumb is one tab stop with aria-valuenow / min / max.
- `ArrowRight / ArrowUp` increases by one step
- `ArrowLeft / ArrowDown` decreases by one step
- `PageUp / PageDown` increases / decreases by a larger step
- `Shift+Arrow` larger step
- `Home / End` minimum / maximum

ARIA in the source: role=slider · aria-disabled · aria-label

## Usage

Only inside a Slider.

Presentational: the Slider gives it role="slider" through Radix.
