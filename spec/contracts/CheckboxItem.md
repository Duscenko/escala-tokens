## Behaviour

**Checkbox** (radix-ui, checkbox). The box is one tab stop; its Label is the accessible name.
- `Space` toggles checked (indeterminate → checked)

ARIA in the source: role=checkbox · role=group · aria-describedby · aria-disabled · aria-invalid · aria-labelledby

## Usage

Use standalone (terms, opt-ins) or as a row of a CheckboxGroup. Placement right for settings lists where the label leads. The description explains the consequence; keep the label short.

## Accessibility

The row is a `<label>` for the box, so a click anywhere toggles it and the label is its name; the description is aria-describedby.
