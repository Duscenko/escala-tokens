# Split Bar — component contract

> GENERATED by `npm run gen:contracts` from `spec/contracts/SplitBar.json` — never edit. No authored contract in design-plans/ yet; this view stands in for it.

A single bar that is 100 % of something, cut into shares (SplitBarSegment) over a legend (SplitBarLegendItem) that turns each series on and off. The bar clips its segments to Radius/action; the legend wraps on Spacing/gap-group.

**Category** Indicators · **Box** gray · **Figma set** none (codeOnly) · [Storybook](https://escala-storybook.vercel.app/?path=/story/indicators-splitbar--playground)

| `leaderBelow` | `number` | A share under this percent (default 5) keeps its text out of the bar: a callout with a leader line sits under it. |
| `onSegmentClick` | `(id: string) => void` | A segment was pressed. |

## Usage

Parts of one whole, up to eight: traffic by source, spend by team, storage by type. Colours are Chart/series-1…8 (categories with no meaning); when a share means good or bad, use Status/* instead. A share that is not part of a whole is a ProgressLine; a trend is a Sparkline. Related: SplitBarSegment, SplitBarLegendItem, ProgressLine.

## Accessibility

role="group" named by the chart title. Each segment is a button named "label, share, value unit"; each legend item an aria-pressed toggle. Tab visits segments then legend items; Enter or Space presses. Colour is never the only divider: every segment has a Chart/series-n/solid edge.
