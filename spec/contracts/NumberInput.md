## Behaviour

**Own source** (spinbutton). The value is the only tab stop; the Decrease / Increase buttons are not tab stops and turn disabled at min / max.
- `ArrowUp` adds one step
- `ArrowDown` subtracts one step
- `Enter` commits the typed value
- Source: src/components/escala-ui/number-input.tsx · the spinbutton onKeyDown · story `Playground`

ARIA in the source: role=spinbutton · aria-invalid · aria-label · aria-valuemax · aria-valuemin · aria-valuenow

## Usage

Use for small numeric ranges (quantities, counts, seats). For wide ranges prefer Slider or a plain Input. Labels and hints come from Field.

## Accessibility

The value is role="spinbutton" with aria-valuenow, aria-valuemin and aria-valuemax; Arrow Up and Down step it. The buttons are labelled Decrease and Increase and are not tab stops. Give the field a label (Field, or aria-label).
