## Behaviour

**DropdownMenu** (radix-ui, menu-button). Roving focus inside the menu; the trigger keeps aria-expanded in sync.
- `Space / Enter / ArrowDown` opens the menu from the trigger and focuses the first item
- `ArrowDown / ArrowUp` moves between items (wraps)
- `Home / End` first / last item
- `Enter / Space` activates the focused item and closes the menu
- `Escape` closes the menu and returns focus to the trigger
- `A–Z` typeahead to the item starting with the letter

ARIA in the source: role=menu · role=separator · aria-checked · aria-disabled

## Usage

Clear / Apply or a single action. Inline puts the text first and the buttons at the end; stacked gives full-width buttons.

## Accessibility

Keep it outside role menu; its controls are reachable with Tab after the list.
