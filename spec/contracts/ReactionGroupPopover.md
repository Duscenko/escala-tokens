## Behaviour

**Popover** (radix-ui, dialog-non-modal). Focus moves into the content on open and back to the trigger on close; outside click dismisses.
- `Space / Enter` opens / closes from the trigger
- `Escape` closes and returns focus to the trigger
- `Tab` moves through the popover content

ARIA in the source: role=group · role=status · role=toolbar · aria-disabled · aria-expanded · aria-haspopup · aria-hidden · aria-label · aria-orientation

## Usage

Only as the popover of a ReactionGroupTrigger. It opens above the trigger and closes when an option is picked.

## Accessibility

role="toolbar" with a roving tabindex: arrow keys move and wrap, Home and End jump, Escape closes and returns focus to the trigger. Every option has an aria-label.
