## Behaviour

**Switch** (radix-ui, switch). One tab stop, role="switch" with aria-checked.
- `Space / Enter` toggles on / off

ARIA in the source: role=switch · aria-describedby · aria-disabled · aria-invalid · aria-label

## Usage

Use for a setting that applies at once. For a choice submitted with a form, use Checkbox. Thumb long makes a wider, pill-shaped thumb; the I / O marks and a thumb glyph help where colour alone is not enough.

## Accessibility

Radix switch (role="switch", aria-checked, Space toggles). Give it a visible label (SwitchGroup) or an accessible name.
