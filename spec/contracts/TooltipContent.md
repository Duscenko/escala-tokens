## Behaviour

**Tooltip** (radix-ui, tooltip). Opens on hover after a delay and on keyboard focus of the trigger; never focusable itself; the trigger is described by it.
- `Escape` hides the tooltip

ARIA in the source: role=tooltip

## Usage

Use for a short label or hint that never carries the only copy of information or an action. Variant must match its trigger.

## Accessibility

role="tooltip", referenced from the trigger by aria-describedby; it opens on hover and keyboard focus and closes with Escape.
