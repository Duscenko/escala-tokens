## Usage

Use one size per context. Stack people with AvatarGroup. A photo avatar has no colour variants; initials and icon take Variant and Appearance.

## Accessibility

role="img" with an accessible name (aria-label, or the alt of the photo); hide a purely decorative avatar with aria-hidden. The presence dot is announced as text.
