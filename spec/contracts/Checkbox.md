## Behaviour

**Checkbox** (radix-ui, checkbox). The box is one tab stop; its Label is the accessible name.
- `Space` toggles checked (indeterminate → checked)

ARIA in the source: role=checkbox · role=group · aria-describedby · aria-disabled · aria-invalid · aria-labelledby

## Usage

Use for independent on/off choices and multi-select lists; indeterminate for a parent whose children are partly checked. For an instant setting, use Switch. Variant colours the checked box (default accent, secondary neutral, status colours, inverse on dark surfaces).

## Accessibility

Radix checkbox (role="checkbox", aria-checked mixed for indeterminate, Space toggles). Give it a label: CheckboxItem, or a Label with htmlFor.
