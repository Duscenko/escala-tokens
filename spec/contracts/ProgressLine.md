## Behaviour

**Progress** (radix-ui, progressbar). Not focusable; aria-valuenow / aria-valuemax announce the value (indeterminate when no value).

ARIA in the source: role=progressbar · aria-valuemax · aria-valuemin · aria-valuenow

## Usage

Use for operations that take more than a couple of seconds and report how far along they are. Pair it with a label and the percentage in the surrounding UI. For an unknown duration use Spinner.

## Accessibility

role="progressbar" with aria-valuenow, aria-valuemin and aria-valuemax; give it an accessible name (aria-label).
