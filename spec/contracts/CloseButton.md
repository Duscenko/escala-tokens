## Usage

Use in the corner of anything the person can put away. Always give it an aria-label (it defaults to "Close"); use the inverse variant on an inverse surface such as a tooltip or snackbar. A dismiss that needs words is a Button.

## Accessibility

Native `<button>` with an aria-label (there is no visible text), "Close" by default. Disabled sets aria-disabled. The glyph is decorative (aria-hidden).
