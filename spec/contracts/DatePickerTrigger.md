## Motion

- `root`: border-color → `use-hover`, box-shadow → `use-focus`

## Behaviour

**Popover** (radix-ui, dialog-non-modal). Focus moves into the content on open and back to the trigger on close; outside click dismisses.
- `Space / Enter` opens / closes from the trigger
- `Escape` closes and returns focus to the trigger
- `Tab` moves through the popover content

ARIA in the source: aria-busy · aria-disabled · aria-haspopup · aria-label

## Usage

Use to pick one date without leaving the form. Pair it with a Label (Field), open DatePickerPopover from it and put the chosen date back as its value.

## Accessibility

A real button with aria-haspopup="dialog"; its text is the chosen date, so no separate label is read. Expose the error through aria-describedby.
