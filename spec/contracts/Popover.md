## Motion

- enter: `popover-enter` on `root`
- exit: `popover-exit` on `root`

## Behaviour

**Popover** (radix-ui, dialog-non-modal). Focus moves into the content on open and back to the trigger on close; outside click dismisses.
- `Space / Enter` opens / closes from the trigger
- `Escape` closes and returns focus to the trigger
- `Tab` moves through the popover content

ARIA in the source: aria-label

## Usage

Open on click, not hover. Side is the trigger edge it opens from. Keep one primary action inside at most.

## Accessibility

Trigger gets aria-haspopup + aria-expanded. Escape or an outside click closes it and focus returns to the trigger. Label it with aria-labelledby on the title.
