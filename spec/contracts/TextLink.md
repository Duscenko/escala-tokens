## Usage

Use inside running copy and next to a field or a card for a destination or a small action. Give it a descriptive label; an icon-only link needs an aria-label. Hover and Pressed always show the underline; the underline can be turned off at rest. An action with its own surface is a Button.

## Accessibility

Native `<a>`. Disabled sets aria-disabled, tabindex -1 and swallows the click. Focus draws a 2px edge inside and a 2px halo outside (the link grows by the edge, as in the Figma file). An icon-only link needs an aria-label.
