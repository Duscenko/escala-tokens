## Behaviour

**RadioGroup** (radix-ui, radio-group). The group is one tab stop with roving focus.
- `Tab` enters the group on the checked item (or the first)
- `ArrowDown / ArrowRight` checks and focuses the next item (wraps)
- `ArrowUp / ArrowLeft` checks and focuses the previous item (wraps)
- `Space` checks the focused item

## Usage

Use for one choice among 2–5 visible options. For more, use Select.

## Accessibility

Radix radio group: arrow keys move the selection, one tab stop. Wrap it in a FieldSet with a FieldLegend.
