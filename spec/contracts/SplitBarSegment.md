## Usage

Inside a SplitBar, never alone. Its width is its share of 100 %, set by the bar. Label shows when it fits, the value alone when it is narrower, nothing under that.

## Accessibility

A button. The visible text is aria-hidden: give it an aria-label with label, share and value. Focused draws a Stroke/focus ring inside the segment in Border/ring/neutral.
